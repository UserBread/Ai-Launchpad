# Design System

## Direction
Apple-inspired editorial product storytelling: strong typography, generous whitespace, restrained palette, clear hierarchy, high-quality surfaces, and purposeful motion. Do not use Apple logos, proprietary assets, or copy exact page layouts.

## Tokens
- Background: `#f5f5f7`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Text: `#171717`
- Secondary text: neutral grey
- Accent: `#0878f9`
- Large radius: 24–32px
- Content width: approximately 1280px

## Components to extract as the site grows
- `SiteHeader`, `SiteFooter`
- `PrimaryButton`, `SecondaryButton`
- `Eyebrow`, `SectionHeading`
- `FeatureCard`, `ModelCard`, `ToolCard`, `WorkflowCard`
- `SearchBar`, `FilterChip`, `Tag`
- `WorkflowNode`, `WorkflowConnector`

## Interaction principles
- Keep navigation simple and predictable.
- Use motion to reinforce state, not decorate everything.
- Provide visible focus states and keyboard access.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid low-contrast grey text for important content.
