# Product Vision

## One-line vision
An AI launchpad for discovering, understanding, and eventually composing AI tools, models, and workflows.

## Problem
AI products and models are fragmented across providers, categories, pricing pages, and documentation. People struggle to discover the right capability and understand how tools fit together.

## Initial audience
- Curious users who want to understand the AI landscape
- Developers choosing models and AI tooling
- Creators looking for repeatable workflows
- Teams evaluating AI capabilities

## MVP goals
1. Establish a distinctive and trustworthy visual identity.
2. Explain the product in seconds.
3. Create a clear information architecture for discovery.
4. Demonstrate the workflow concept without pretending execution exists yet.
5. Make the site responsive and accessible.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Running model inference
- Storing user API keys
- Autonomous agents
- User accounts or billing
- User-submitted catalogue entries

## Future milestones
1. Searchable AI catalogue and curated collections
2. Model/tool profile pages with sourced, timestamped information
3. Comparisons and recommendation guides
4. Workflow template gallery
5. Saved stacks and collections
6. Provider connectors and multi-model playground
7. Workflow builder and execution runtime
8. Community publishing and marketplace

## Product principles
- Useful before clever
- Provider-neutral
- Transparent about data sources and update dates
- Accessible and responsive
- Safe defaults for credentials, permissions, and external actions
